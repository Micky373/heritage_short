# Ethics, consent, and provenance

## Disclosure and provenance

The Amharic narration is synthesized with Gemini TTS. The finished 78-second
film includes a spoken AI disclosure in shot 09 and a separate visual
English/Amharic AI disclosure card. The current character sheet and keyframes
are deterministic vector illustrations authored by the local pipeline; camera
motion is applied to stills with FFmpeg. The score is a deterministic synthetic
placeholder. Do not describe the current visuals or score as model-generated
footage or recorded instruments.

The generated voice uses the configured Gemini TTS voice; it is not presented
as a recording or clone of a real person's voice. Have a fluent Amharic speaker
review pronunciation, meaning, pacing, and the English subtitle translation
before publication.

## People and audience feedback

The storyboard depicts a father, a child, and an adult narrator in a personal
story. If these characters represent identifiable real people, obtain their
informed permission for the story and any likeness before public release.
Because a child is represented, use particular care not to expose identifying
details or imply the child consented on an adult's behalf.

The radio-cut feedback in `DESIGN.md` is summarized under anonymous listener
labels. This repository does not currently document a consent record for
participant names, recordings, or quotations. Do not publish identifying
feedback or recordings without permission. The near-final test protocol in
`AUDIENCE_TEST.md` asks for voluntary, anonymous written feedback and does not
require audio/video recording.

## Data, credentials, and logs

`.env` is ignored and must remain local. Never put API keys or tokens in
`runs.jsonl`, issue reports, or documentation. The tracked `runs.jsonl` does
contain request parameters, including narration text; treat it as project
content, review it before public distribution, and avoid sending sensitive
personal details in prompts.

The generated media and cache are ignored under `outputs/` and `.cache/`.
Review all outputs for accidental identifying information before sharing.

## Licensing and publication

The local illustration and score are generated by project code, rather than
downloaded third-party media. Gemini use remains subject to the provider's
current terms and account settings. The subtitle renderer uses Noto Sans
Ethiopic through the system font configuration; if distributing the font
itself, include and comply with its license. Keep a provenance/license record
for any future external or model-generated assets, including model/version,
prompt, date, seed when available, usage terms, and required attribution.

Before public release, confirm consent, review the final disclosure, verify
asset rights, and check that no credentials or private participant information
are included.
