## Protocol

1. Invite at least three willing viewers. Explain the film's personal subject
   matter and that it contains AI-generated narration before screening;
   participation is voluntary. Do not point out when the visual disclosure
   appears.
2. Show the same near-final 78-second export to each viewer. Record the
   filename, aspect ratio, date, and whether playback was uninterrupted.
3. Use anonymous labels (A, B, C); do not collect contact details. Do not
   record audio/video unless the viewer separately gives explicit permission.
4. Ask the same questions without prompting for positive feedback. Note
   actionable edits and whether each was accepted, deferred, or rejected.
