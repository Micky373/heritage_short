## Audience Test #1 (Radio Cut — `outputs/radio_cut.mp4`)

* **Participants:** Three listeners, anonymized here as Listener A, Listener B, and Listener C.
* **Format:** 75-second radio cut (`outputs/radio_cut.mp4`) featuring the 9-shot Amharic narration track, ambient/placeholder backing, and closing AI disclosure on `shot_09`.

### Listener Takeaways
* **Listener A:** Highlighted the emotional resonance of the Sidist Kilo morning walks, English/math practice on the move, and how those early habits carry into current data work; suggested smoothing brief pauses between shots and giving the closing AI statement a clean, unhurried finish.
* **Listener B:** Praised the warm, grounded tone and personal growth arc from early morning routines to international confidence; recommended keeping mid-cut pacing tight so the link to data science stays effortless to follow.
* **Listener C:** Noted strong narrative focus and a clear bridge from everyday reasoning to data and code; suggested keeping inter-shot gaps brisk while preserving the clear closing AI disclosure.
